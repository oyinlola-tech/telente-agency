# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in this project.

## Our Standards
We expect:
- Professional, respectful communication
- Constructive feedback and thoughtful collaboration
- Accountability and ownership of impact
- Respect for privacy, confidentiality, and intellectual property

We do not tolerate:
- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation
- Trolling, personal attacks, or abusive language
- Unwanted sexual attention
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces including issues, pull requests, discussions, and any private communications related to project work.

## Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer directly and include:
- A description of what happened
- Links or screenshots where possible
- Names of people involved

Reports will be handled discreetly and as promptly as possible.

## Enforcement
Actions may include warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent removal from project spaces, depending on severity and frequency.
