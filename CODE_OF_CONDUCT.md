# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and considerate.
- Assume good intent and give constructive feedback.
- Avoid harassing, discriminatory, or abusive language or behavior.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, bullying, or intimidation.
- Discriminatory jokes or insults.
- Posting private or sensitive information without consent.

## Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer directly.
